# Vantara Private Car Cover - synthetic policy wording

SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Not an insurance contract or an approved insurance product.

Fictional issuer: Vantara General Insurance. Product code: MOT.
Document ID: VGI-MOT-SYN-2026-01. Wording version: 1.0. Corpus version: 1.
Prepared for Dak on 2026-10-19. All amounts, time limits and procedures below are fictional test rules.
The legacy seed product name Comprehensive Motor Insurance maps to this synthetic MOT wording only.

## MOT-C01 | Vehicle and sections selected

This fixture concerns a private car identified in the individual synthetic schedule. Own-damage and third-party liability are different sections; the schedule must show which are present. Having one section does not establish the existence of the other. A general reply can explain the distinction, but it must not declare a customer's vehicle insured for a particular event without checking the recorded cover and event details.

## MOT-C02 | Accidental damage to the insured car

External collision damage, fire, theft and direct damage from a flood or storm are events considered under the own-damage section when that section is selected. Assessment still requires the event to fall within the recorded period and usage conditions. Damage to another person's vehicle is not an own-damage claim for the insured car; third-party matters are considered under MOT-C03.

## MOT-C03 | Third-party liability

The third-party section addresses a demand concerning injury to another person or damage to another person's property arising from use of the scheduled car. A demand letter, police record or legal notice must be forwarded to the reviewer without admitting liability or negotiating a settlement. No universal financial limit is supplied by this fixture; the applicable schedule and human legal assessment are required.

## MOT-C04 | Vehicle value and loss assessment

The recorded insured vehicle value is a schedule-specific ceiling for an own-damage assessment, not a guaranteed settlement. A repair estimate, the condition of the car and the event circumstances are examined by a reviewer. The assistant must not derive the vehicle's value from a registration year, premium, public listing or another synthetic customer's record. The total-loss conclusion is outside automated authority.

## MOT-C05 | Deductibles

The default own-damage deductible in this synthetic wording is INR 1,750 for each assessed event. A voluntary deductible applies only when separately recorded in the schedule. A deductible is a contribution to an assessed loss and is not an extra charge for submitting an email. The assistant may explain the generic default but cannot quote a customer's final settlement without the complete assessment.

## MOT-C06 | Replacement parts

The default replacement-parts deduction in this fixture is 15% of the assessed cost of a replaced component. Labour is not included in that component calculation. A recorded zero-depreciation endorsement can change the treatment only within its own scope. This simplified demo rule is not a representation of any real insurer's depreciation table, and the assistant must not invent age bands or material-specific percentages.

## MOT-C07 | Wear, breakdown and consequential damage

Routine wear, maintenance and a mechanical or electrical fault without a covered external event are outside the base own-damage benefit. Additional damage caused by continuing to use a visibly unsafe vehicle needs separate examination. A breakdown assistance request is different from a repair reimbursement claim. Naming a mechanical component in an email does not itself establish either coverage or an exclusion.

## MOT-C08 | Engine and flood distinction

Direct flood damage to body panels can be considered under the own-damage section. Engine damage caused by attempting to restart a waterlogged car is outside the base engine benefit in this fixture. An explicitly recorded engine-protection endorsement may alter that position and must be inspected by a human. Conflicting event descriptions or endorsements must be retained as uncertainty rather than resolved by the drafting model.

## MOT-C09 | Use and driver conditions

This fixture assumes private use of the scheduled car and a driver holding the required licence. Paid racing, deliberate damage and an event involving an unauthorised usage category require human assessment under the exclusion conditions. The assistant cannot infer intoxication, licence validity or commercial use from tone, a driver's name or an ambiguous description. A disputed factual premise is a reason to request evidence, not to announce rejection.

## MOT-C10 | Theft evidence

A theft intimation should include the policy number, registration number, location, date last seen and any police complaint reference. Standard supporting items are a claim form, registration certificate, available keys and police acknowledgement. A final police report may be requested later by the reviewer. Lack of that later report at initial notification is not an automatic rejection and must not prevent acknowledging receipt.

## MOT-C11 | Accident evidence and inspection

Standard accident evidence comprises a claim form, registration certificate, driver licence, repair estimate and available event photographs. A police record is relevant when injury, a third-party demand or another reportable circumstance is described. The customer should preserve evidence and discuss non-emergency repairs with the reviewer. Necessary steps to protect people must not be delayed to obtain a demo claim acknowledgement.

## MOT-C12 | Optional roadside assistance

Roadside assistance exists only when road_assistance appears in the recorded rider list. The synthetic towing allowance under that rider is a distance of 35 kilometres to an appropriate repair facility. Fuel, accommodation and subsequent vehicle repair charges are not included in this allowance. The assistant must not claim a dispatch has been booked: this application records and reviews messages and has no assistance dispatch integration.

## MOT-C13 | Policy period and waiting periods

Own-damage event eligibility uses the recorded start date, end date and any effective cancellation date. This motor fixture has no illness or pre-existing-condition waiting period. Health waiting-period fields accidentally present on a legacy motor seed row are not motor cover conditions. If a generic calculator applies such a health block, preserve the discrepancy and refer it for correction before a motor assessment is relied upon.

## MOT-C14 | Claims and loss reduction

A customer should report the event promptly with the known facts and protect the vehicle from further avoidable damage where safe. The reviewer assesses any delay and explanation on the evidence. No clause here authorises the assistant to approve a repair, reject a claim, accept fault or instruct a garage to begin work. Claim acknowledgement is separate from authorisation and from settlement.

## MOT-C15 | No-claim history and renewal queries

A no-claim history is customer-specific and must be checked against the policy and claims records. This fixture supplies no universal discount table, renewal price or promise that a discount will transfer. An enquiry about a renewal quote can be acknowledged and referred. The assistant must not calculate a quote or claim that a previous year's cover has automatically renewed.

## MOT-C16 | Complaint and disputed assessment

A complaint about valuation, a delay or an exclusion should be checked against the claim record and the cited wording. If disputed_internally is set on a referenced rejection, the complaint requires human review and a concrete internal note describing the conflict. The customer-facing reply remains neutral. An earlier rejection is historical information, not proof that the same conclusion should be repeated automatically.

## MOT-C17 | Formal demands and human authority

Regulatory references, court papers and lawyer notices are forwarded to a human regardless of confidence in the category. The system must retain the document reference and the reason for escalation without admitting liability. A third-party claimant's requested amount is an allegation to be reviewed, not an amount the company has accepted. No automatic message may promise a legal outcome or a settlement deadline.

## MOT-C18 | Product boundary and attachments

This fixture does not insure goods in transit, commercial fleets, motorcycles, travel disruption or personal investments. Missing evidence is described using the relevant evidence clause rather than a generic request for supporting documents. Dak can inventory attachment names, but it cannot inspect images, verify a licence or certify a police record. A question outside this wording must be referred without inventing another product's terms.
